Yours only

Entries are encrypted on your phone and protected with a PIN or Face ID. No ads, no trackers, no AI reading your entries.

Calm down

Calming sounds with a timer — rain, sea, forest — keep playing with the screen off. Breathing exercises: box breathing, 4-7-8, even breathing and long exhale.
